Two things no other task board does

Team Rescue Mode

If a member leaves or becomes unavailable, GroupMate works out what is affected and proposes a recovery plan for your team to approve.

  1. Finds their unfinished tasks and anything that depends on them.
  2. Compares the rest of the team’s skills, availability and workload.
  3. Suggests new owners and an adjusted schedule.

Recover from disruption without missing your deadline.

Assessment Gap Detector

Upload your assignment brief or marking rubric. GroupMate pulls out the requirements, matches them to your planned tasks and flags anything with no task or evidence.

Find forgotten requirements while there is still time to fix them.
